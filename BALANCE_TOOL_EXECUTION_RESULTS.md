## Balance Observations

### Strengths ✅
1. **Smooth Early Game** - First 6 hours show good progression pacing
2. **Clear Milestones** - Generators and upgrades unlock at regular intervals
3. **Extension Scaling** - Red Generator Extensions scale reasonably (40 min to 6 hours)
4. **Prestige Timing** - Yellow prestige at ~2.6 days feels appropriate for major reset

### Areas for Consideration ⚠️
1. **Accelerator Unlock** - 20+ hours to unlock may feel long
2. **Late Booster Levels** - Levels 4-5 taking 1.5-2 days each
3. **Star Particle Grind** - 4-11 days for star particle milestones is very long
4. **Yellow Content Gap** - Large gap between yellow prestige (2.6d) and next major content

### Recommendations 💡
1. **Consider** reducing unlock time for Red Accelerators from 20 hours to 12-15 hours
2. **Monitor** player feedback on Booster Acceleration 4-5 timing
3. **Evaluate** if star particle progression (4-11 days) provides sufficient engagement
4. **Add** intermediate content between yellow prestige and 50% star particles
