Targets

Blue combiners are very powerfull and can accelerate the game a lot. There are two different items to be bought here: Subjects and Targets. Subjects are mostly currencies you have and Targets are things like upgrades and generators. Beneath the subjects and targets are the combining boxes containing two sides: the Subject side and the Target side. With the 'Assign' button, a subject and target can be assigned to the box. When a combiner box is assigned a subject and target, the target will gain a multiplier based on the subject

Hint: you can play around with combinations to see which one works the best.
